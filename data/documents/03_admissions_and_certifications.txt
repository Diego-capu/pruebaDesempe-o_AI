TechUni Admissions Process, Industry Certifications, and Accreditation

1. Admissions Requirements and Application Process
- Undergraduate Admissions (B.Sc. Programs): Requires High School Diploma / Upper Secondary Certificate (official translated transcript), Mathematics Proficiency (passing score in Calculus/Algebra or TechUni Math Prep Module), valid Government ID or Passport copy, and submitted Online Application Form.
- Master of Science Admissions (M.Sc. Programs): Requires a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or related technical field, minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0/4.0, updated CV, 1-page Statement of Purpose (SOP), and a technical interview with the Program Director.
- Application Deadlines for Fall Semester: Early Action Deadline is May 15; Regular Deadline is August 1.
- Application Deadlines for Spring Semester: Early Action Deadline is October 15; Regular Deadline is January 10. Late applications processed on rolling space-available basis.

2. Industry Certifications Included in Curriculum
TechUni integrates official vendor certification pathways into regular course work at no additional cost:
- AWS Academy Accredited Curriculum: Prepares students for AWS Certified Solutions Architect (Associate) and AWS Certified Machine Learning (Specialty) exams. TechUni provides 50% to 100% exam voucher discount codes.
- Cisco Networking & Security Certification: Coursework aligned with Cisco CCNA and CyberOps Associate certifications.
- Google Cloud & NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute: Hands-on certificates embedded in M.Sc. and Bootcamp modules.

3. Accreditation, Credit Transfer, and Degree Validation
- Institutional Accreditation: Fully accredited by the National Higher Education Quality Assurance Council (NHEQAC) and international engineering recognition standards.
- Credit Transfer: Up to 60 credit hours transferred from accredited post-secondary institutions, subject to syllabus review by the Academic Credit Committee.
- International Apostille & Degree Validation: TechUni issues official English transcripts, diploma supplements, and European Credit Transfer System (ECTS) equivalency documents.
