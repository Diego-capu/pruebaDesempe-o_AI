1. ADMISSIONS REQUIREMENTS & APPLICATION PROCESS

A. Undergraduate Admissions (B.Sc. Programs)
- High School Diploma / Upper Secondary Certificate (official translated transcript).
- Mathematics Proficiency: Passing score in High School Calculus or Algebra, or completion of TechUni Math Prep Module.
- Valid Government ID or Passport copy.
- Online Application Form submitted through admissions portal.

B. Master of Science Admissions (M.Sc. Programs)
- Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or closely related technical field.
- Minimum undergraduate cumulative GPA of 3.0/4.0.
- Updated Curriculum Vitae (CV) and 1-page Statement of Purpose (SOP).
- Technical interview with Program Director.

C. Application Deadlines
- Fall Semester Admission: Early Action Deadline is May 15; Regular Deadline is August 1.
- Spring Semester Admission: Early Action Deadline is October 15; Regular Deadline is January 10.
- Late applications are processed on a rolling space-available basis.

2. INDUSTRY CERTIFICATIONS INCLUDED IN CURRICULUM
TechUni integrates official vendor certification pathways into regular course work at no additional cost:

A. AWS Academy Accredited Curriculum
- Students in AI & Data Engineering and CyberCloud programs prepare for AWS Certified Solutions Architect (Associate) and AWS Certified Machine Learning (Specialty) exams.
- TechUni provides 50% to 100% exam voucher discount codes.

B. Cisco Networking & Security Certification
- Coursework aligned with Cisco CCNA and CyberOps Associate certifications.

C. Google Cloud & NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute
- Hands-on certificates embedded in M.Sc. and Bootcamp modules.

3. ACCREDITATION & DEGREE VALIDATION
- Institutional Accreditation: Fully accredited by the National Higher Education Quality Assurance Council (NHEQAC) and international engineering recognition standards.
- Credit Transfer: Up to 60 credit hours transferred from accredited post-secondary institutions, subject to syllabus review by the Academic Credit Committee.
- International Apostille & Transcript Validation: TechUni issues official English transcripts, diploma supplements, and European Credit Transfer System (ECTS) equivalency documents for graduates seeking international employment or further postgraduate study.
