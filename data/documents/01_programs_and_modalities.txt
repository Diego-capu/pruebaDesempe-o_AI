1. ACADEMIC OFFERINGS & DEGREES
TechUni offers specialized degree programs designed for technical and engineering careers:

A. Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence & Data Engineering (B.Sc. AI & Data)
- Duration: 4 years (8 semesters), total 140 credit hours.
- Core Focus: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, MLOps, Big Data Pipelines, Neural Networks, Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing.
- Capstone Project: Applied AI industry project in Year 4.

B. Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity & Cloud Systems (B.Sc. CyberCloud)
- Duration: 4 years (8 semesters), total 138 credit hours.
- Core Focus: Network Defense, Ethical Hacking, Cloud Infrastructure (AWS/Azure/GCP), DevSecOps, Cryptography, and Zero Trust Architecture.

C. Master of Science in Software Architecture & Autonomous Systems (M.Sc. Software Arch)
- Duration: 2 years (4 semesters), total 48 credit hours.
- Core Focus: Distributed Systems, Microservices, Agentic AI Workflows, System Resilience, High-Performance Computing.
- Thesis or Capstone Industry Practicum option.

D. Executive Technical Bootcamps (Certificates)
- Duration: 6 months (intensive part-time).
- Topics: Full-Stack Web Development with React/FastAPI, Cloud DevOps Engineering, Data Engineering & Analytics.

2. STUDY MODALITIES
TechUni provides three flexible learning modalities to accommodate full-time students and working professionals:

A. On-Campus (100% Presential)
- Classes held at Main Campus (Tech Tower, Science Park District).
- Hands-on access to High-Performance GPU Supercomputing Labs and Hardware Cyber Labs.
- Attendance requirement: Minimum 80% lab and lecture presence.

B. Hybrid (Blended Learning)
- Theoretical lectures delivered synchronously online (50%).
- Practical lab sessions and exam assessments conducted on-campus (50%).
- Designed for local commuting students.

C. 100% Online (Asynchronous + Live Mentorship)
- Video lectures accessible 24/7 via the TechUni Learning Portal.
- Weekly live Q&A webinars with professors on Saturday mornings.
- Remote virtual GPU desktop environments provided for coding and labs.
