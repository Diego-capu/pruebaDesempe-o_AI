TechUni Academic Programs, Degrees, and Study Modalities

1. Academic Offerings and Degrees
Technological University of Advanced Science & Engineering (TechUni) offers specialized degree programs:

- Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence & Data Engineering (B.Sc. AI & Data): Duration of 4 years (8 semesters), total 140 credit hours. Core Focus includes Machine Learning, Deep Learning, MLOps, Big Data Pipelines, Neural Networks, Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing. Includes an Applied AI industry Capstone project in Year 4.
- Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity & Cloud Systems (B.Sc. CyberCloud): Duration of 4 years (8 semesters), total 138 credit hours. Core Focus includes Network Defense, Ethical Hacking, Cloud Infrastructure (AWS/Azure/GCP), DevSecOps, Cryptography, and Zero Trust Architecture.
- Master of Science in Software Architecture & Autonomous Systems (M.Sc. Software Arch): Duration of 2 years (4 semesters), total 48 credit hours. Core Focus includes Distributed Systems, Microservices, Agentic AI Workflows, System Resilience, and High-Performance Computing. Offers Thesis or Capstone Industry Practicum option.
- Executive Technical Bootcamps (Certificates): Duration of 6 months (intensive part-time). Covers Full-Stack Web Development with React/FastAPI, Cloud DevOps Engineering, and Data Engineering & Analytics.

2. Study Modalities
- On-Campus (100% Presential): Classes at Main Campus (Tech Tower, Science Park District). Hands-on access to High-Performance GPU Supercomputing Labs and Hardware Cyber Labs. Minimum 80% attendance required for labs and lectures.
- Hybrid (Blended Learning): Theoretical lectures delivered synchronously online (50%). Practical lab sessions and exam assessments conducted on-campus (50%).
- 100% Online (Asynchronous + Live Mentorship): Video lectures accessible 24/7 via TechUni Learning Portal. Weekly live Q&A webinars with professors on Saturday mornings. Remote virtual GPU desktop environments provided for coding and labs.

3. Class Schedules and Shifts
- Morning Shift: Monday to Friday, 08:00 AM - 12:30 PM (On-Campus & Hybrid).
- Evening Shift: Monday to Friday, 06:00 PM - 09:45 PM (Hybrid & Online).
- Weekend Shift: Saturdays, 08:30 AM - 05:00 PM (Executive Bootcamps & M.Sc. programs).
- Academic Calendar: Fall Semester (September - January), Spring Semester (February - June), Summer Intensive (July - August).
