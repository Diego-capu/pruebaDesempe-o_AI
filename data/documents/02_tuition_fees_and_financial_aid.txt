TechUni Tuition Fees, Payment Plans, Scholarships & Financial Aid

1. Tuition and Registration Fee Structure (Academic Year 2026-2027)
- Admissions Application Fee: $75 USD (non-refundable one-time fee).
- Enrollment Seat Deposit: $250 USD (one-time deposit credited toward first semester tuition upon confirmation).
- Undergraduate Programs (B.Sc. AI & Data / B.Sc. CyberCloud): Full-Time Tuition (15-18 credits per semester) is $4,200 USD per semester. Part-Time Tuition (under 12 credits) is $320 USD per credit hour. Annual total for 2 semesters is $8,400 USD.
- Master of Science Programs (M.Sc. Software Arch): Tuition per semester (12 credits) is $3,600 USD. Total Program Cost across 4 semesters is $14,400 USD.
- Executive Technical Bootcamps: Total Bootcamp Fee is $1,800 USD flat rate for the full 6-month program.

2. Payment Flexibility and Installment Plans
- Single Early Payment Discount: 8% discount on total annual tuition if paid in full at least 30 days prior to semester start.
- Monthly Installment Plans (3-Pay & 5-Pay): Interest-free semester payment split. The 5-Pay Plan requires a 20% down payment at registration, followed by 4 monthly payments on the 5th of each month. Processing fee is $25 USD setup fee per semester.
- Corporate Sponsorship & Employer Billing: TechUni invoices approved corporate employers directly. Deferred payment extended up to 30 days post-term upon submission of corporate voucher.

3. Scholarships and Financial Aid Programs
- Future Tech Innovators Merit Scholarship: Up to 50% tuition reduction for top applicants with a minimum GPA of 3.8/4.0 (or 90/100 equivalent) and high score on TechUni Admissions Logic Assessment.
- Women in STEM & Tech Leadership Grant: $1,500 USD per year tuition award for female applicants enrolled in AI, Data Engineering, or Cybersecurity programs.
- Financial Need Assistance Grant: 20% to 35% tuition assistance based on verified household income evaluation.

4. Cancellation and Refund Policy
- Cancellation before Day 1 of classes: 100% tuition refund (excluding non-refundable $75 application fee).
- Withdrawal during Weeks 1-2: 80% tuition refund.
- Withdrawal during Weeks 3-4: 50% tuition refund.
- Withdrawal after Week 4: No refund issued for current semester.
