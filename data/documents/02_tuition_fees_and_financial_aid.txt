TECHNOLOGICAL UNIVERSITY OF ADVANCED SCIENCE & ENGINEERING (TECHUNI)
DOCUMENT 02: TUITION FEES, PAYMENT PLANS, SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL AID
===================================================================

1. TUITION & REGISTRATION FEE STRUCTURE (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)

A. One-Time Application & Registration Fees
- Admissions Application Fee: $75 USD (non-refundable).
- Enrollment Seat Deposit: $250 USD (credited toward first semester tuition upon confirmation).

B. Undergraduate Programs (B.Sc. AI & Data / B.Sc. CyberCloud)
- Full-Time Tuition (15-18 credits per semester): $4,200 USD per semester.
- Part-Time Tuition (under 12 credits): $320 USD per credit hour.
- Annual Total (2 semesters): $8,400 USD.

C. Master of Science Programs (M.Sc. Software Arch)
- Tuition per semester (12 credits): $3,600 USD.
- Total Program Cost (4 semesters): $14,400 USD.

D. Executive Technical Bootcamps
- Total Bootcamp Fee: $1,800 USD (flat rate for the full 6-month program).

2. PAYMENT FLEXIBILITY & INSTALLMENT PLANS

A. Single Early Payment Discount
- 8% discount on total annual tuition if paid in full at least 30 days prior to semester start.

B. Monthly Installment Plan (3-Pay & 5-Pay)
- Students can split semester tuition into equal monthly payments without interest.
- 5-Pay Plan: 20% down payment at registration, followed by 4 monthly payments on the 5th of each month.
- Processing fee: $25 USD setup fee per semester.

C. Corporate Sponsorship & Employer Billing
- TechUni invoices approved corporate employers directly. Deferred payment extended up to 30 days post-term upon submission of corporate voucher.

3. SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL AID PROGRAMS

A. Future Tech Innovators Merit Scholarship
- Coverage: Up to 50% tuition reduction for top applicants.
- Eligibility: Minimum GPA of 3.8/4.0 (or 90/100 equivalent) and high score on TechUni Admissions Logic Assessment.

B. Women in STEM & Tech Leadership Grant
- Coverage: $1,500 USD per year tuition award.
- Eligibility: Female applicants enrolled in AI, Data Engineering, or Cybersecurity programs.

C. Financial Need Assistance Grant
- Coverage: 20% to 35% tuition assistance based on verified household income evaluation.

4. CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY
- Cancellation before Day 1 of classes: 100% tuition refund (excluding non-refundable $75 application fee).
- Withdrawal during Weeks 1-2: 80% tuition refund.
- Withdrawal during Weeks 3-4: 50% tuition refund.
- Withdrawal after Week 4: No refund issued for current semester.
